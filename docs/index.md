# Wizard Manager 

Write ingredient lists and instructions for spells and potions! 
 ![Wizard1](https://i.pinimg.com/736x/d2/44/3e/d2443ec3dc7ab1548631fbd1ff17e837.jpg)

## Example for Intermediate level alchemists:

 ### Ingredients for Knight Repellent

  Medium sized Cauldron
  
  The Liver of a Frog

  An ounce of Hair from an albino newt

  Apple Cider Vinegar

  A live Goat

  Tusk of a Pygmy Elephant

  An victim's Wifi Router
  
  DO NOT THROW IN CAULDRON:

  Sorcerer of Shadow's Midnight Scepter

### Instructions

  1. Take your medium-sized cauldron, fill ~halfway with water and set over a fire.
  
  2. Fill frog liver with newt hair and leave to soak in Apple Cider Vinegar

  3. Add live Goat to Cauldron after it has begun to simmer

  4. Throw in the finished frog liver

  5. Break Wifi Router with the Midnight Scepter and throw Router in DO NOT ADD SCEPTER TO CAULDRON

  6. Stir brew with tusk until tusk dissolves.

  7. Elixir on tower to ward off Knights
