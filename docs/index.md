# Wizard Manager 

Doth thou forget how to brew thine elixirs? Doth thine draw casting circles end up without essential runes?
Write ingredient lists and instructions for spells, potions and more!

## Example for Intermediate level alchemists:

 ### Ingredients for Knight Repellent

  Medium sized Cauldron
  
  The Liver of a Frog

  An ounce of Hair from an albino newt

  Apple Cider Vinegar

  A live Goat

  Tusk of a Pygmy Elephant

  An unknowing participant's Wifi Router
  
  DO NOT THROW IN CAULDRON:

  Sorcerer of Shadow's Midnight Scepter

### Instructions

  1. Take thine medium-sized cauldron, fill halfway with water and set over a fire to simmer.
  
  2. Fill frog liver with newt hair and leave to soak in Apple Cider Vinegar

  3. Add live Goat to Cauldron

  4. Throw in fermented frog liver

  5. Break Wifi Router with the Midnight Scepter and throw Router in DO NOT ADD SCEPTER TO CAULDRON

  6. Stir brew with tusk until tusk dissolves.

 Elixir should look like this upon completion:
 
![](https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcQ3DAe3Hs1Mws23a3bJW2ci28NlJt1po_-PSJQcHUHSbg&s=10)

Spray once daily to ward off knights.
If being used to ward off the King's chosen Hero, replace wifi router with same victim's airfryer(if not available take other appliance)
